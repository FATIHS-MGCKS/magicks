# MAGICKS Design & Voice Rules

## Brand Direction

MAGICKS is a premium studio for digital presences, web software, and automations.

The brand should feel:
- premium
- calm
- clear
- business-oriented
- editorial
- cinematic
- precise
- trust-building

MAGICKS should not feel like:
- a generic web agency
- a colorful template agency
- a SaaS dashboard template
- a loud startup landing page
- an overdone AI agency
- a design studio without business impact

Core positioning:
MAGICKS does not simply build websites. MAGICKS creates digital presences that explain, build trust, and generate inquiries.

## Design Rules

### Color System

MAGICKS uses a warm, light, premium visual system.

Preferred colors:
- warm off-white
- ivory
- paper tones
- light beige
- sand
- deep ink black
- champagne / brass accents

Rules:
- Color must support hierarchy, trust, and clarity.
- Accents must be used sparingly.
- Avoid neon colors, loud gradients, cold pure white as the dominant look, random section-by-section color changes, and colorful generic agency visuals.

### Typography

Use a precise sans-serif foundation for UI, body copy, navigation, descriptions, and buttons.
Use the editorial serif only for large statements, emotional highlights, and premium headline moments.
Use mono/label styling only for subtle chapter markers or metadata.

Rules:
- Sans = clarity and trust.
- Serif = editorial premium emphasis.
- Avoid too many font styles, too many font weights, oversized letterspacing on mobile, and serif for long explanatory copy.

### Layout

MAGICKS uses an editorial layout language:
- generous whitespace
- clear section rhythm
- strong hierarchy
- calm compositions
- fine lines
- precise cards
- subtle side labels
- premium spacing

Rules:
- Each homepage section must have a clear role in the story.
- Not every section should look like a hero.
- Avoid repeated centered giant headlines, generic grids, random mockups, and overloaded visual effects.

Recommended homepage story:
1. Hero -- positioning and first impression
2. Value Statement -- why digital impact matters
3. Problem / Reality -- what many websites fail to do
4. Services -- what MAGICKS offers
5. Why MAGICKS -- differentiation and trust
6. Process -- how the work happens
7. Proof / Projects / Trust -- credibility
8. CTA -- clear next action

### Motion

Motion is part of the MAGICKS brand.

Motion should feel like:
- camera movement
- scroll dramaturgy
- high-end editorial motion
- soft transitions
- subtle depth

Allowed:
- subtle reveals
- fade / blur transitions
- masked text reveals
- slow parallax movement
- ScrollTrigger choreography
- calm device/mockup movement
- subtle hover states

Avoid:
- bounce effects
- hectic transitions
- constantly moving elements
- motion that harms readability
- mobile animations that feel abrupt or laggy

Rules:
- Mobile motion must be calmer than desktop motion.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Motion must never damage performance.

### Image Direction

Images should be realistic, premium, calm, and business-relevant.

Preferred:
- high-end photography
- real studio or workspace feeling
- premium devices
- realistic screens
- clear composition
- natural shadows
- subtle technical details

Avoid:
- generic stock people
- neon AI robots
- abstract 3D worlds without business relevance
- hologram clichés
- cheap-looking website screens
- unrealistic device mockups

Service image logic:
- Websites & Landingpages: premium digital presence, strong interface, conversion focus.
- Shops & Konfiguratoren: product, selection, configuration, purchase decision.
- Web-Software: order, process clarity, dashboard, operational relief.
- KI & Automationen: structured workflows, less manual work, intelligent systems.

### Component Style

Components must feel:
- precise
- calm
- premium
- structured
- minimal but not empty
- conversion-oriented

Avoid:
- cheap shadows
- overloaded cards
- unnecessary borders
- playful hover effects
- inconsistent spacing
- generic button styles

CTA examples:
- Projekt anfragen
- Erstgespräch starten
- Website prüfen lassen
- Zusammenarbeit besprechen

Avoid:
- Jetzt loslegen!!!
- Bring dein Business aufs nächste Level
- Kostenlos durchstarten
- Jetzt unverbindlich revolutionieren

## Voice Rules

### Tone

MAGICKS speaks in a clear, premium, direct, business-oriented voice.

The tone should be:
- confident, not arrogant
- creative, but purposeful
- premium, but understandable
- precise, not cold
- conversion-focused, not pushy

Avoid:
- generic agency language
- over-poetic wording
- exaggerated AI language
- empty marketing phrases

### Formal Address

Use formal German address consistently:
- Sie
- Ihr
- Ihre
- Ihnen

These must always be capitalized on the website.

### Copy Logic

Copy should usually follow:
Problem → Impact → Solution → Result

Example:
Viele Websites sehen gut aus, erklären aber zu wenig. MAGICKS entwickelt digitale Auftritte, die Vertrauen schaffen, Leistungen verständlich machen und aus Besuchern echte Anfragen machen.

Avoid:
Wir erstellen moderne Websites mit kreativem Design.

### Sentence Structure

Use short, precise sentences.
One thought per sentence.
Avoid long nested agency sentences.

Good examples:
- Der erste Eindruck entscheidet. Der zweite bleibt.
- Eine Website muss nicht nur sichtbar sein. Sie muss überzeugen.
- Wir verbinden Design, Entwicklung, SEO und Automationen zu einem digitalen Auftritt, der für Ihr Unternehmen arbeitet.

Avoid:
Als ganzheitlicher Partner für digitale Transformation erschaffen wir maßgeschneiderte Erlebniswelten, die Marken nachhaltig skalieren.

### Preferred Words

Use:
- überzeugen
- Vertrauen
- Wirkung
- Klarheit
- Anfragen
- Umsatz
- Sichtbarkeit
- Prozesse
- digitaler Auftritt
- Verkaufsfläche
- Web-Software
- Automationen
- aus einer Hand
- messbar
- verständlich
- hochwertig
- strategisch
- professionell

Use sparingly:
- Magie
- Erlebnis
- KI
- Innovation
- Transformation
- digital
- modern
- kreativ

Avoid:
- 360° Lösungen
- Next Level
- disruptive Innovation
- maßgeschneiderte digitale Ökosysteme
- Ihre Vision ist unsere Mission
- Wir zaubern
- Wir machen Sie erfolgreich
- modern, kreativ und professionell without a concrete benefit

### Core Brand Lines

These lines are approved as MAGICKS brand language:
- Nicht nur online. Überzeugend.
- Digitale Erlebnisse, die begeistern.
- Mehr als Websites.
- Aus Sichtbarkeit wird Vertrauen. Aus Vertrauen werden Anfragen.
- Design, Entwicklung, SEO und Automationen aus einer Hand.
- Wir gestalten digitale Auftritte, die nicht nur gut aussehen, sondern für Ihr Unternehmen arbeiten.
